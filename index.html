Movies

Keeping track of films and shows. No account, no server: everything stays in your browser, and you can export a backup any time.

Watchlist & Review Both in one place. Your watchlist, with a thumb up or down once you’ve seen a title, and a review mode for swiping through curated lists of films and shows. Starts empty: import an export from the classic pages below to bring your titles over.

Classic pages

Watchlist What to watch next. Search movies and TV, add YouTube videos, sort by priority, tag by mood or platform, mark titles watched and review them with stars, notes and a rewatch flag. Ratings What you actually like. Swipe through curated lists of films (most watched, Oscar nominees, decades, genres) and TV shows with a thumb up or down, add stars and notes, and build a taste profile of the genres, themes and eras you lean toward.

Working together

All three pages keep their own data and use the same export file, so an export from any of them imports into the others. Imports merge: nothing is removed, and the more recently changed version of each title wins.
