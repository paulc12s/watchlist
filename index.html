Movies

Two small apps for keeping track of films. No account, no server: everything stays in your browser, and each app can export a backup.

Watchlist What to watch next. Search movies and TV, add YouTube videos, sort by priority, tag by mood or platform, mark titles watched and review them with stars, notes and a rewatch flag. Ratings What you actually like. Swipe through curated lists of films (most watched, Oscar nominees, decades, genres) with a thumb up or down, add stars and notes, and build a taste profile of the genres, themes and eras you lean toward.

Working together

Films you mark “want to watch” in Ratings export as a file the Watchlist imports, and Ratings can import a Watchlist export to pick up the stars and notes you’ve already given.
